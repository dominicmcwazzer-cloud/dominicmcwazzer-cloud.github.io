6ix AM Club

It started with a question: what would a Mamelodi run club's brand look like as an actual running app? Book the run, count down to the start line, track it, show up on the board. 15 screens, 4 flows, built on the brand the club already made.

That idea became a series: a new drop every week. Concepts for local and international brands, personal projects and ideas I just want to make. Any digital medium: apps, motion, 3D, 2D animation, graphics, editorial, whatever the idea needs.
